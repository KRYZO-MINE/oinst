Good to know

How does the formatting work?

Responses are parsed and walked recursively. Objects become label/value rows, arrays of records become numbered cards, simple lists become chips, and URLs, emails, phone numbers, dates and images are rendered as the right element automatically.

Do I need the raw data?

Every result keeps a “Show raw response” toggle with syntax-highlighted JSON, plus one-click copy and download as .json.

Where are the keys stored?

In Vercel’s environment variables, read only by the serverless proxy. Locally, use a git-ignored .env.local file.
